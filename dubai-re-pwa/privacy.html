Privacy Policy

Prototype notice: have counsel review before production use.

What we process

Queries you type or dictate, plus preferences and an audit log stored on your device.

Protection

Emails, phone numbers, Emirates ID numbers, passport-style IDs, IBANs and card numbers are redacted before a query is sent. Local data is encrypted with AES-GCM using a non-exportable key and deleted after 30 days.

Third parties

Redacted queries are sent to a Gumloop AI agent workflow for processing.

Access, portability and erasure are available on the Compliance tab.
